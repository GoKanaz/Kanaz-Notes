# Privacy Policy for KanazNotes

This privacy policy applies to the **KanazNotes** app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, created by **GoKanaz** (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Open Source service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

---

### 1. Privacy-First Approach
KanazNotes is designed to be a private-by-default note-taking tool. Unlike conventional applications, we do not monitor, collect, or store your personal data on any central servers.

* **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII).
* **No Tracking or Analytics:** The Application does not use any third-party SDKs, usage statistics, or analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase) to track user behavior.
* **No Automatic Data Harvesting:** We do not gather your IP address, device ID, or hardware information.

### 2. Data Storage and Security
#### Local Storage
All notes, titles, and content created within the Application are stored locally on your device's internal storage. The Service Provider has no access to your data.

#### PIN Protection
The Application includes an optional security feature to protect your notes with a PIN or password.
* **Encryption:** Security credentials are encrypted and stored locally on your device.
* **Zero-Knowledge:** We cannot recover your PIN if it is lost, as this information is never transmitted to us or any third party.

### 3. Optional WebDAV Synchronization
KanazNotes provides an optional synchronization feature using the open WebDAV protocol.
* **User-Controlled:** Data is transmitted directly from your device to the specific WebDAV server that you have personally configured.
* **No Intermediaries:** We do not own, manage, or have access to your chosen server.
* **Credential Safety:** Your login credentials (username/password) are stored locally and used only for direct authentication with your server. They are never logged or monitored by the Service Provider.

### 4. Permissions
The Application may request specific permissions to function correctly:
* **Internet Access:** Used solely for the optional WebDAV synchronization feature.
* **Storage Access:** Used for importing/exporting backups or saving local databases.

### 5. Third-Party Access and Disclosure
Since we do not collect any data, we do not share, sell, or trade your information. We will only disclose information if required by law to comply with a legal process or to protect safety/rights, though we possess no user data to provide in most circumstances.

### 6. Children's Privacy
The Application does not knowingly collect or solicit any information from children. It is inherently safe for users of all ages as no data is harvested.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating this page.
